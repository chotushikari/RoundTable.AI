# 15 — ASSESSMENT & EVALUATION

## Separate live interviewing from deep assessment

Live:
- fast
- concise
- stateful
- robust

Post-interview:
- slower
- deeper
- evidence aggregation
- cross-answer consistency
- recommendation

## Scoring model

For each competency:
- belief/score
- confidence
- supporting evidence
- concerns
- unresolved questions

## Evidence rule

No high-confidence conclusion without supporting evidence.

## Example

Technical reasoning:
88/100
Confidence: High

Evidence:
- 03:12 verbal architecture explanation
- 04:05 failure-mode discussion
- 11:42 code/test result

Customer impact:
57/100
Confidence: Medium

Concern:
Candidate initially made a customer-impact claim without a measurable outcome; later provided a metric after Product probing.

## Recommendation

Use labels such as:
- Strong Hire
- Hire
- Lean Hire
- Lean No
- No Hire

But always show the evidence profile and confidence.

## Candidate feedback

Feedback should be:
- specific
- transcript-linked
- role-aware
- actionable

Bad:
“You need to communicate better.”

Good:
“In the initial system-design answer, your architecture was strong, but the customer outcome was not quantified. After a Product follow-up, you identified checkout conversion as the relevant metric.”

## Fairness

Do not use:
- accent
- appearance
- facial expression
- demographic inference
- speech speed
as direct competence scores.
