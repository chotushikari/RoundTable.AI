# 03 — PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

## 1. Continuous, not level-based

Do not implement “Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 3” as the core interview model.

Maintain continuous evidence and uncertainty across competencies.

Example:
- Technical reasoning: strong
- Customer impact: weak evidence
- Communication: strong
- Ownership: uncertain

## 2. Every follow-up needs a reason

No arbitrary “tell me more.”

Examples:
- missing metric
- missing mechanism
- missing trade-off
- missing customer impact
- contradiction
- incomplete implementation
- unverified claim

## 3. Role changes must feel motivated

Good:
> “Your implementation makes sense. I want to explore what that means for the customer.”

Bad:
> “Switching to Product Agent…”

## 4. The candidate remains in control of the conversation

Allow:
- interruptions
- clarification
- “I don't know”
- disagreement
- requests to restate constraints

## 5. Evidence beats impression

Do not score accent, appearance, enthusiasm, filler words, or emotion as proxies for capability.

## 6. Candidate and judge views are different

Candidate:
- calm
- voice-first
- minimal cognitive overhead

Judge/recruiter:
- evidence
- state
- rationale
- timing
- metrics
- assessment

## 7. Reliability beats feature count

A stable adaptive loop is more valuable than ten partially functioning features.
