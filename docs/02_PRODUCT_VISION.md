# 02 — PRODUCT VISION

## Product

**RoundTable AI — Adaptive Interview Intelligence**

## Positioning

An adaptive, multimodal AI interview room where specialist interviewers operate over one evolving evidence model of the candidate.

## The product is not

- A question generator
- A voice-enabled questionnaire
- A collection of independent personas
- A resume score generator
- A coding copilot disguised as an interviewer

## The product is

A system that continuously asks:

1. What has this candidate actually demonstrated?
2. What remains uncertain?
3. What evidence would reduce that uncertainty?
4. Which interviewer is best positioned to obtain that evidence?
5. Should the next interaction be voice, code, debugging, scenario, role-play, or clarification?
6. When is there enough evidence to stop?

## Product north star

Maximize **useful, job-relevant evidence per minute** while preserving a natural interview experience.

## Primary value

For employers:
- consistent structured assessment
- deeper evidence than scripted screens
- cross-functional perspective
- reduced repetitive first-round workload

For candidates:
- realistic conversation
- adaptive questions
- actionable feedback
- opportunity to demonstrate ability in multiple modalities

## Long-term platform direction

The interview engine should be connector-aware. MCP can provide read-only real-world context from systems such as GitHub, Linear, PostHog or other approved tools. The connector is not the product; the product is the adaptive interview that uses the context intelligently.
